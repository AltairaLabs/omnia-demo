OUTSOURCING AGREEMENT: FIRST NOTIFICATION OF LOSS

Between Larkmere Mutual (the "Society") and Fernside Claims Services (the "Supplier").
Fictional: all names, figures and terms are invented for a demonstration.

1. Services. The Supplier will take first notification of loss calls for household claims, 24 hours a day.
2. Fees. A monthly fee of £18,500, invoiced in advance and payable within 30 days.
3. Term and renewal. This agreement runs for 12 months and renews automatically for further periods of 36 months unless either party gives at least 9 months' written notice before the end of the current period.
4. Termination for breach. Either party may end this agreement at once if the other commits a material breach and fails to remedy it within 30 days.
5. Data protection. The Supplier will process member personal data only on the Society's documented instructions.
6. Liability. Each party's total liability is limited to the fees paid in the 12 months before the claim.
7. Governing law. This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales.
