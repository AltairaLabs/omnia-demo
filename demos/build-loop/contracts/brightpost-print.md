SUPPLY AGREEMENT: PRINT AND MAILING SERVICES

Between Larkmere Mutual (the "Society") and Brightpost Print Services (the "Supplier").
Fictional: all names, figures and terms are invented for a demonstration.

1. Services. The Supplier will print and post member statements and renewal packs as ordered by the Society.
2. Fees. The Society will pay the Supplier's invoices within 30 days of receipt, at the rates in Schedule 1.
3. Term. This agreement starts on 1 January 2027 and runs for 24 months.
4. Termination. Either party may end this agreement on 90 days' written notice.
5. Data protection. The Supplier will process member personal data only on the Society's written instructions and will tell the Society of any breach within 24 hours.
6. Indemnity. The Society shall indemnify the Supplier in full against all losses, claims, costs and expenses of any kind arising out of or in connection with this agreement, without limit.
7. Liability. The Supplier's total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees paid in the 12 months before the claim.
8. Governing law. This agreement is governed by the law of England and Wales.
