# After a flood: what happens to your claim

Larkmere Mutual guidance CP-7, for members whose home has been flooded: water
coming in from outside (rivers, surface water, the sea or groundwater), not a
leak from inside the home.

## Flood claims: who handles them

Flood claims are handled by our Flood Response team. A flood rarely affects
one home, so the team looks at claims in the same area together, and checks
whether the property is in a mapped flood zone and covered by Flood Re.

## Flood claims: what happens next

After a flood claim is referred to Flood Response, the team contacts the
member within one working day. If the flooded home can't be lived in, the team
can arrange emergency accommodation under the policy's alternative
accommodation cover. Once the visit is done, Flood Response arranges drying
and repairs through our approved contractors, or settles the flood claim.

## Flood claims: what the member should do now

Until Flood Response has been in touch, a member with a flood claim should:
take photos and video of the flood damage and the water line before anything
is cleared, if it is safe to; keep damaged items if possible, or photograph
them before they go; not start drying or repairs unless it is needed to stop
more damage; and keep receipts for anything spent because of the flood.
