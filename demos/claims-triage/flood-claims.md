# After a flood: what happens to your claim

Larkmere Mutual guidance CP-7, for members whose home has been flooded: water
coming in from outside (rivers, surface water, the sea or groundwater), not a
leak from inside the home.

## Who handles it

Flood claims are handled by our Flood Response team. A flood rarely affects
one home, so the team looks at claims in the same area together, and checks
whether the property is in a mapped flood zone and covered by Flood Re.

## What happens next

- Flood Response contacts the member within one working day.
- If the home can't be lived in, the team can arrange emergency
  accommodation under the policy's alternative accommodation cover.
- The team arranges drying and repairs through our approved contractors, or
  settles the claim, once the visit is done.

## What the member should do now

- Take photos and video of the damage and the water line before anything is
  cleared, if it is safe to.
- Keep damaged items if possible, or photograph them before they go.
- Don't start drying or repairs before Flood Response has been in touch,
  unless it is needed to stop more damage.
- Keep receipts for anything spent because of the flood.
