# Flood claims procedure

Larkmere Mutual claims procedure CP-7: claims where the cause is flood, meaning
water entering the home from outside (rivers, surface water, the sea or
groundwater), not escape of water from inside it.

## The rule

Every flood claim goes to the Flood Response team, whatever its amount. An
automated assessor does not settle a flood claim, even one within delegated
authority, because a flood claim is rarely the only one: the team checks
whether neighbouring members are affected and whether the property is in a
mapped flood zone before anything is paid.

## What the assessor does

1. Refer the claim with claim_refer, to the Flood Response team, giving the
   reason "Flood claim: CP-7 requires Flood Response review".
2. In the note, record the cause, the amounts assessed and payable, and that
   the claim was referred under CP-7.

## What the Flood Response team does

- Contacts the member within one working day.
- Checks the flood zone and the Flood Re eligibility of the policy.
- Settles or arranges repairs, and records whether neighbouring claims were
  linked.
