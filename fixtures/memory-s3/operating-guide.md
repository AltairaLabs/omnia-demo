# Operating guide

Member claims and mortgage applications are handled by separate operational
teams, but both use the same audit requirements. Every decision must include a
case reference, the source evidence considered, the policy version, and the
identity of the person or service that made the decision.

Claims above the delegated authority limit require human approval. A missing or
ambiguous document is a reason to pause and request evidence, not a reason to
infer the missing facts. Search results are references; the current policy
record is authoritative for the final decision.
