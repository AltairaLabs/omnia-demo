# Single-phase meter exchange (WE-MET-03)

Wrenmoor Energy field manual WE-MET-03. Exchanging a single-phase domestic
meter at the customer's premises. Low-voltage work at the cut-out; the
Wrenmoor Energy Safety Rules still apply.

## Before you start

1. Tell the customer the supply will be off for about 30 minutes.
2. Inspect the cut-out before touching anything. If it is cracked, scorched,
   shows heat marks or has a damaged seal, stop. Do not exchange the meter.
   Raise a cut-out defect job and make the installation safe.
3. Record the old meter's serial number and final reading, and photograph the
   meter and its seals.

## Exchange

1. Isolate the supply by withdrawing the cut-out fuse carrier. Prove dead at
   the meter terminals with an approved voltage indicator, proved before and
   after on a known live source.
2. Remove the old meter. Check the meter tails for damage; replace tails
   shorter than the new meter's terminal spacing allows.
3. Fit the new meter and torque the terminals to the value on the meter label.
4. Refit the cut-out fuse carrier and restore supply.

## After the exchange

1. Carry out a polarity check at the meter before you reseal. A reversed
   polarity is corrected before you leave.
2. Confirm the new meter registers by switching on a load at the premises.
3. Fit new seals to the meter and the cut-out and record the seal numbers.
4. Record the new meter's serial number and opening reading on the job record.
