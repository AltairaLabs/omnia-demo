# Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions. For the full policy on any topic, see the linked detailed article.

## Shipping and delivery

**When will my order arrive?**
Standard shipping is 5-7 business days from when your order leaves our warehouse. Orders placed before 1pm Pacific on business days typically ship the same day. Free on orders over $75. See `shipping-policy.md`.

**Do you offer free shipping?**
Yes, on any domestic US order over $75. Calculated after discount codes are applied.

**Do you ship internationally?**
Yes, to most countries. Customer pays duties. See `international-shipping.md`.

**How do I track my order?**
You get a tracking email when the carrier picks up your package. See `order-tracking.md`.

**My tracking hasn't updated in days. What do I do?**
If it's been more than 2 business days, contact support. See `order-tracking.md`.

**Tracking says "delivered" but I don't have it.**
Check with neighbors, building managers, and safe-drop locations first, wait 24 hours, then contact support if still missing. See `order-tracking.md`.

## Returns and refunds

**What's your return policy?**
30 days from delivery. Items must be in original condition with tags. Sale items are final sale. See `returns-and-exchanges.md`.

**How do I return something?**
Contact support and we'll email you a prepaid return label within one business day.

**How long does a refund take?**
1-2 days for us to inspect after we receive it, then 5-7 days for your bank to process. Total round trip is typically 2-3 weeks.

**Do you do exchanges?**
Yes, exchanges are free. The new item ships as soon as we receive the original.

**Can I return a sale item?**
No. Sale items are final sale. We flag this clearly at checkout.

**The item arrived damaged. What do I do?**
Contact support with a photo. You get a refund or replacement (your choice), we pay return shipping, 60-day window. See `damaged-or-incorrect-items.md`.

**I got the wrong item. What do I do?**
Same as damaged — contact support, we'll ship the correct item and send a prepaid return label.

## Sizing and fit

**How does your sizing run?**
True to size for most categories. Denim runs slightly small. Outerwear runs generous. See `sizing-guide.md`.

**I'm between sizes. Which should I pick?**
Tops and dresses: smaller for fitted, larger for relaxed. Denim: larger. Outerwear: smaller. See `sizing-guide.md`.

**The size I ordered doesn't fit. Can I exchange it?**
Yes, free exchange within 30 days. See `returns-and-exchanges.md`.

**Do you have a size chart?**
Yes, women's and men's size charts are in `sizing-guide.md`.

**How does Acme sizing compare to [other brand]?**
Most similar to Everlane, Quince, and Cuyana. If you size medium in those, medium in Acme. See `sizing-guide.md`.

## Care and materials

**How do I wash this?**
Always check the care label first. General guidance for linen, cotton, denim, wool, and cashmere is in `care-instructions.md`.

**Will this shrink?**
Pre-washed cotton: minimal. Untreated cotton: 2-4%. Wool/cashmere: significant if you machine wash (don't). See `care-instructions.md`.

**Can I put this in the dryer?**
Most items are better air-dried. Cotton tolerates tumble drying low. Wool and cashmere should NOT go in the dryer. See `care-instructions.md`.

**What fabrics do you use?**
We use mostly natural fibers: linen, cotton, wool, cashmere, silk. We avoid synthetic blends wherever possible. Fabric composition is listed on every product page.

## Orders and account

**How do I cancel my order?**
If your order hasn't shipped yet, contact us immediately and we'll cancel it. Once an order has shipped, we can't cancel — but you can return it after delivery.

**Can I change my shipping address after I've ordered?**
Only if the order hasn't shipped yet. Contact us immediately.

**Can I combine orders?**
Usually no — orders ship separately as soon as they're placed. If you placed two orders within minutes of each other and neither has shipped, contact us and we may be able to combine them.

**Do you have a loyalty program?**
Not currently. We may add one in the future.

**Do you offer gift cards?**
Yes. Gift cards are delivered by email, don't expire, and can be used in any amount on any future order.

**I have a discount code. How do I apply it?**
Enter it at checkout before completing the order. Discount codes are single-use unless otherwise noted, and cannot be combined with other codes.

## Payment and security

**What payment methods do you accept?**
All major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover), Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay, PayPal.

**Is my payment information secure?**
We don't store your card details on our servers. All payment processing is handled by Shopify and Stripe, both of which are PCI DSS compliant.

**Something looks wrong on my statement.**
Contact us with your order number and we'll look into it. **If you believe it's a duplicate charge or fraud, also contact your bank** — but let us know so we can escalate on our side.
