# Damaged or Incorrect Items

If your order arrived damaged, defective, or we sent the wrong item — we're sorry and we'll make it right. This article covers the process and what to expect.

## What counts as a damaged or incorrect item

**Damaged**:
- Tears, rips, holes, or cuts that weren't there when it left us
- Broken zippers, loose buttons that should be fastened, snapped straps
- Stains or discoloration that look like manufacturing defects (not customer-caused)
- Water damage from the shipping container

**Defective**:
- Seams coming apart, stitching failures, loose threads unraveling
- Fabric defects: missing weave, obvious manufacturing flaws
- Failed hardware: zippers that won't zip, buttons sewn wrong
- Color inconsistencies between the product photo and what you received (beyond reasonable monitor variation)

**Incorrect**:
- Wrong size from what you ordered
- Wrong color from what you ordered
- Wrong style or item entirely
- Missing items from a multi-piece order

## What you get

For any of the above:

- **Full refund OR free replacement** (your choice)
- **We pay return shipping** (prepaid label emailed within one business day)
- **No restocking fee**
- **60-day window** for defective items (not the standard 30-day return window)
- **For damaged items**: we may ask for photos before approving — see below

## The process

1. **Contact us** — reply to your order confirmation or chat with support. Describe the issue.
2. **Send us photos** (for damaged items) — a clear photo of the damage and the full item helps us process the claim quickly and file a claim with the carrier if needed
3. **We approve within one business day** — in most cases, we don't need much from you beyond a description and a photo
4. **Pick your resolution** — refund or replacement
5. **For replacements**: we ship the correct item immediately (free expedited shipping), and send you a prepaid return label for the wrong/damaged item. You can return the original at your convenience — no deadline pressure on our side.
6. **For refunds**: ship the item back using the prepaid label; refund processes within 5-7 business days after we receive it.

## What if I don't want to deal with returning the wrong item?

For small items under $50, we'll often just **let you keep it** and ship the replacement. This is at our discretion and we'll let you know when we're offering this option. It's a nice-to-have, not a guarantee.

For larger items, we do need the original back — but we make it easy with a prepaid label and no deadline.

## Specific scenarios

### "The color looks different in person"

Monitor color accuracy varies a lot. If a navy looks more "dark blue" than you expected, or a beige looks more "tan," that's usually not something we'd classify as a defect — it's normal monitor variation. If you're not sure, send us a photo and we'll give you an honest assessment. If the color is genuinely different from the product page, we'll treat it as incorrect.

### "It doesn't fit / it's not what I expected stylistically"

This is a standard return, not a defect. See `returns-and-exchanges.md` for the 30-day return process. You'll pay return shipping unless you're exchanging for a different size of the same item.

### "It arrived with a wear defect" (light pilling, minor thread loose)

Some fabrics pill lightly out of the bag — wool and cashmere especially. This isn't a defect. One or two loose threads on a new garment are also normal. If the defect is more significant than that (visible hole, large area of pilling, major thread separation), treat it as defective.

### "The seam popped after one wear"

That's defective. Seams shouldn't fail on the first wear. Contact us with a photo and we'll send a replacement — and we'd appreciate knowing which style it was so we can flag it for quality review.

### "I was shipped the wrong thing entirely"

Contact us immediately. We'll ship the correct item same-day if possible, and send a prepaid label for the incorrect one. We'll also look into how it happened on our end.

### "Something was missing from a multi-item order"

Sometimes one item in a multi-piece order is out of stock when we go to pack it and gets split to a later shipment. You should get an email explaining this when it happens. If you didn't get that email and something is missing, contact us — we'll track down whether it's a shipping issue or a fulfillment error.

## What we will NOT do

- **Process a refund on a used item** and claim it's defective. We inspect every return; worn items don't qualify.
- **Accept photos of third-party damage** (package damaged by a pet, a partner, a kid, etc.). We're sorry, but we can't cover that.
- **Expedite a replacement for a "damaged" item that isn't defective.** Monitor color variation, preference issues, style disagreements — those are standard returns.

We trust our customers by default, but we do investigate patterns. One damaged-item claim is no big deal. Five in a row from the same customer triggers a conversation.
