# Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you get a tracking email with everything you need. Here's what to expect and what to do if tracking looks wrong.

## When you get your tracking email

You'll get a tracking email **when the carrier picks up your package from our warehouse**, not when you place the order. The gap is typically:

- **Orders placed before 1pm Pacific on a business day**: tracking email within a few hours, same day
- **Orders placed after 1pm Pacific or on weekends/holidays**: tracking email by end of the next business day

If you placed your order more than 2 business days ago and still haven't received a tracking email, check your spam folder first. If it's not there, contact support — there may be a holdup we need to look into.

## Reading your tracking email

The tracking email contains:

- A **tracking number** (16-22 digit code, depending on carrier)
- A **carrier link** — click it to see live tracking on the carrier's website (USPS, UPS, or FedEx)
- **Estimated delivery window** — usually a 2-3 day range, not a single date
- A **"Having issues?" link** that takes you back to our support page

## Tracking status meanings

Carriers use different terminology, but they mean similar things:

| Carrier status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Label created / Shipping label ready** | We've generated the label but the package hasn't been picked up yet |
| **In transit / Out for delivery** | The package is on its way |
| **Out for delivery** | The package is on the truck for delivery today |
| **Delivered** | The carrier has dropped it off |
| **Delivery attempted** | The carrier tried to deliver but couldn't (no answer, blocked access, etc.) |
| **Exception** | Something is wrong — weather delay, customs hold, damaged package, etc. |
| **Returned to sender** | The package is coming back to us, usually because nobody picked it up at a carrier facility |

## What to do if tracking looks stuck

Tracking updates can go quiet for 1-2 days during transit, especially for packages traveling between carrier facilities. If tracking has been unchanged for **more than 2 business days**, contact support — we'll file a trace with the carrier and look into it.

**Please don't file a trace yourself** before contacting us. We have direct merchant tools that can resolve stuck packages faster than the customer-facing carrier website.

## What to do if tracking says "delivered" but you don't have it

This happens sometimes. Steps:

1. **Check with neighbors** — some carriers leave packages at neighboring addresses by mistake
2. **Check with your building manager or mail room** — if you live in an apartment or condo, packages sometimes go to a common area or front desk
3. **Wait 24 hours** — occasionally "delivered" scans happen before the actual delivery, especially with USPS
4. **Check for a safe-drop location** — carriers sometimes leave packages behind fences, bushes, or garage areas

If after 24 hours the package is still missing, **contact support immediately**. We'll file a carrier trace and, if the package is confirmed lost, we'll ship a free replacement or refund you (your choice). Don't wait a week — lost-package claims are easier to resolve within the first few days.

## What to do if tracking is stale and we're approaching the delivery window

If your delivery window is tomorrow and tracking still says "in transit" in a different state, it might be a carrier update lag. But it also might be a real delay. Contact us — we'll check the carrier's back-end (not just the customer-facing tracking page) and give you an honest answer.

**We'll never tell you "it'll be there soon" unless we've actually checked.**

## Shipping delays we've seen before

- **Weather holds**: winter storms, hurricanes, flooding — carriers pause delivery in affected regions
- **Carrier sorting facility backups**: especially around major holidays
- **Address problems**: typos in the street number, unit number missing, etc. Carrier will sometimes get through this without issue, sometimes not
- **Customs delays (international)**: import inspections can add 3-10 days

For any delay that's not resolved within 2 business days of the quoted window, we'll either expedite a replacement or offer an EXPRESS_UPGRADE voucher for your next order. See `shipping-policy.md` for our full delay policy.
