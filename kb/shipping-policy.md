# Shipping Policy

All Acme Apparel orders ship from our West Coast fulfillment center in California. Here's everything you need to know about getting your order.

## Shipping rates and timing (domestic US)

| Option | Cost | Delivery time |
|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | Free on orders over $75, otherwise $6.95 | 5-7 business days |
| **Express** | $14.95 | 2 business days |
| **Overnight** | $29.95 | Next business day (order by 1pm Pacific) |

Delivery times are from the day your order leaves our warehouse, not from the day you place the order. Orders placed before 1pm Pacific on business days typically ship the same day; orders placed later ship the next business day. We don't ship on weekends or US federal holidays.

## Free shipping

Any domestic US order over **$75** qualifies for free standard shipping. The threshold is calculated after any discount codes are applied, not before — so if you use a 20% off code that brings your order from $85 to $68, you no longer qualify for free shipping on that order. The threshold is domestic only; international orders don't qualify regardless of order size.

## Which carriers we use

We use USPS, UPS, and FedEx depending on the destination and shipping speed. You don't choose — we pick the fastest and most reliable option for your specific address. Tracking information will tell you which carrier has your package.

## Shipping to PO Boxes

Standard shipping only. Express and overnight options are not available to PO Boxes because they use carriers that don't deliver there. If you need expedited shipping, you'll need to provide a street address.

## Delays and disruptions

Sometimes shipments run late for reasons outside our control — weather, carrier backups, inspection holds, lost scans. If your order is tracking-delayed by more than 2 business days past the expected window, contact us and we'll either expedite a replacement or offer an EXPRESS_UPGRADE voucher for your next order.

**We always check the tracking status before promising a new delivery date.** We never guess or tell customers "it should be there soon" without confirming.

## What happens if you're not home

All carriers we use will attempt delivery up to three times. After the third attempt, packages are usually held at a local carrier facility for pickup (you'll get an email with pickup instructions). If the package is returned to us because nobody picked it up, we'll refund you minus the original shipping cost — please contact us before reordering so we can help.
