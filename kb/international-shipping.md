# International Shipping

We ship to most countries outside the US. Here's what to expect.

## Countries we ship to

- **North America**: Canada, Mexico
- **Europe**: UK, Ireland, France, Germany, Netherlands, Belgium, Spain, Italy, Switzerland, Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland, Austria, Portugal, Poland, Czechia
- **Asia-Pacific**: Japan, South Korea, Singapore, Hong Kong, Australia, New Zealand
- **Middle East**: UAE, Israel

If your country isn't listed, we may still ship there on a case-by-case basis — contact support and we'll check whether our carriers have a reliable path.

## Countries we do NOT ship to

We don't currently ship to mainland China, Russia, most African countries, or most of South America. This is either because our carriers don't have reliable service there or because customs complications make the experience poor for customers. We're working on expanding this list.

## International shipping costs

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on destination, package weight, and chosen speed. Free shipping thresholds do NOT apply to international orders. Typical ranges:

| Destination | Standard cost | Express cost |
|---|---|---|
| Canada | $15-25 | $35-50 |
| UK / EU | $25-40 | $55-85 |
| Asia-Pacific | $30-55 | $70-110 |

These are rough ranges — actual cost depends on your specific address and order size.

## Delivery times

- **Standard international**: 10-14 business days
- **Express international**: 3-5 business days

Delivery times start when the package leaves our US warehouse, not when you place the order. International packages can also sit in customs for anywhere from a few hours to several days depending on the country — this is outside our control.

## Customs, duties, and taxes

**You (the customer) pay any duties, VAT, or import taxes charged by your country.** These are not included in our shipping costs, not included in the product price, and not something we can estimate accurately because they depend on your country's rules.

For some countries, the carrier collects duties at delivery (you pay the driver). For others, the carrier contacts you to pay before delivery. For others, customs may hold the package until you contact them directly. Please be prepared for this — international packages that are refused or abandoned due to unpaid duties cannot be refunded for shipping.

**We cannot mark international shipments as "gifts"** to help customers avoid duties. This is both illegal and unfair to the carriers we work with.

## What to expect

1. You place your order and pay shipping at checkout (no duties yet)
2. We ship from our California warehouse
3. Your package clears US export, travels to your country, clears your country's customs
4. Your carrier (usually the local postal service or a partner of FedEx/UPS) contacts you if duties are owed
5. You pay duties and the package is released for delivery
6. Package arrives

## Returns on international orders

Same 30-day return window and same return conditions as domestic orders. Key difference: **you pay return shipping** (unless the item is defective). You may also need to use a commercial carrier if your local postal service doesn't offer international tracking — contact support and we'll advise.

**Duties paid at import are NOT refundable by us.** This is a function of your country's customs, not our policy. You'll need to contact your local customs authority to ask about a duty refund, and most countries do not issue them for non-commercial returns.

For defective items, we cover return shipping and the replacement ships with duties pre-paid where possible — though this varies by country.

## Sizing for international customers

Our sizing is US standard. If you're in the UK, EU, or Asia and want to match your local sizing, see the conversion chart in `sizing-guide.md`. As a rough rule:

- **UK → US (women's)**: subtract 4 (UK 10 = US 6)
- **EU → US (women's)**: subtract 30 (EU 38 = US 8)
- **JP → US (women's)**: subtract 4 (JP 9 = US 5, approximately)

These are approximate — check the full size chart for accuracy.
