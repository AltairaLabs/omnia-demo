Omnia demo catalogue

Every demo on this install, by tenant. Each tenant is its own Omnia workspace, so the same person has different pseudonyms in each, nothing is shared between them, and each has its own budget. Links open the dashboard scoped to that workspace.

The numbers are an order to show them in, from familiar ground to what only a platform does: 1–2 an agent run properly, 3–5 guardrails you can't prompt your way to, 6–8 it knows your customers safely, 9–10 change it without breaking it, 11 learn from it, 12 build your own. Held means built and waiting on an Omnia fix.
