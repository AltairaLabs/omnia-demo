# Contacts and escalation for field staff

Wrenmoor Energy field operations. Who to call, and when.

## Network Control Centre (NCC)

The NCC directs all HV switching, records every safety document, and decides
when a faulted circuit is re-energised. Call the NCC on the radio, or on
0306 999 0142, staffed 24 hours a day.

Call the NCC when:

- a switching schedule is needed or something unexpected happens during
  switching;
- you need an Authorised Person to issue, transfer or cancel a safety document;
- you find defective switchgear, a damaged cut-out, or third-party damage to
  the network;
- the job will run past the time you gave the NCC.

## Danger to life

Make the area safe without putting yourself at risk, call 999, then call the
NCC. Report near misses the same day through the safety reporting app.

## Customer issues

Customers asking about a power cut can check the Wrenmoor Energy outage map or
call 105, the national power cut number. Field staff do not give restoration
times; the NCC publishes them.
