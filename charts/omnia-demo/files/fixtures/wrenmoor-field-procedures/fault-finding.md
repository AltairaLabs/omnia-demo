# Fault finding on 11kV cables and overhead lines (WE-FLT-02)

Wrenmoor Energy field manual WE-FLT-02. Locating and clearing faults on the
distribution network.

## First response

1. Get the fault details from the Network Control Centre: the feeder, the
   protection that operated, the fault current recorded, and how many
   customers are off supply.
2. For an overhead line, patrol the section before any test: look for broken
   conductors, damaged insulators, vegetation contact and blown fuses.
3. For an underground cable, check for third-party damage first (recent
   excavation, a contractor on site).

## Locating a cable fault

1. The section must be isolated, proved dead and earthed under a Permit to
   Work (WESR section 4) before any test equipment is connected. Testing that
   needs earths removed is done under a Sanction for Test.
2. Measure insulation resistance on each core with a 5kV insulation tester.
   Below 1 megohm to earth on any core confirms a cable fault.
3. Pre-locate the fault with time-domain reflectometry (TDR) from both ends
   where possible.
4. Pinpoint with the surge generator and acoustic locator. Do not surge-test
   above 16kV on 11kV polymeric (XLPE) cable.

## Restoring supply

Supply to healthy sections is restored by the NCC by back-feeding through
normally open points, before the faulted section is repaired. Report the
faulted section's limits to the NCC as soon as you have them.

## Reporting

Record the fault location, the cause, the tests done and their readings on the
job record before you leave site.
