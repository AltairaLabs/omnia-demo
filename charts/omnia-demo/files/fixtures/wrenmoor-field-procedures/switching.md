# Switching on the 11kV network (WE-SW-01)

Wrenmoor Energy field manual WE-SW-01. How HV switching is planned, authorised
and carried out on the distribution network.

## Who authorises switching

All HV switching is directed by the Network Control Centre (NCC). A field
engineer carries out a switching operation only when the NCC control engineer
instructs it, step by step, against an agreed switching schedule. A switching
schedule is never improvised on site.

## Before switching

1. Confirm you hold the competence authorisation for the switchgear type
   (ring main unit, pole-mounted recloser, or primary circuit breaker).
2. Agree the switching schedule with the NCC and confirm its reference number.
3. Check the switchgear for defects: oil leaks, damaged insulation, gas
   pressure (SF6 units) below the green band, or a defect label. Defective
   switchgear is not operated; report it to the NCC.
4. Wear the arc-rated PPE the schedule specifies, at least 12 cal/cm2 for
   ring main units.

## During switching

- Carry out one step at a time, as instructed by the NCC.
- Repeat each instruction back to the control engineer before you act on it,
  and report the result before the next step.
- Stand to the side of the switchgear, never in front of the operating
  mechanism.
- If anything unexpected happens (a trip, a flash, an alarm), stop, step away
  and report it to the NCC. Do not continue the schedule.

## Restoring supply after a fault

Re-energising a faulted section is an NCC decision. A circuit that has tripped
on protection may be closed again only once by the NCC, and only after the
section has been patrolled or the fault located (see WE-FLT-02).
