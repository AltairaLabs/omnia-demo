# Isolation and safety documents (WESR section 4)

Wrenmoor Energy Safety Rules, section 4: Isolation and Earthing. Applies to all
work on high-voltage (HV, above 1kV) equipment on the Wrenmoor Energy
distribution network.

## The rule

No work starts on HV equipment until it has been isolated, proved dead and
earthed, and a safety document has been issued for it by an Authorised Person
(AP). There are no exceptions for time pressure, customers off supply, or
equipment believed to be dead already. A cable that is "almost certainly dead"
is treated as live.

## Safety documents

| Document | Used for | Issued by |
|---|---|---|
| Permit to Work (PTW) | Work on isolated, earthed HV equipment | Authorised Person |
| Sanction for Test (SFT) | Testing that needs earths removed | Authorised Person |
| Limitation of Access (LOA) | Work near, not on, HV equipment | Authorised Person |

Only an Authorised Person can issue, transfer or cancel a safety document. The
Network Control Centre (NCC) records every issue and cancellation. Nobody else,
and no system, can authorise work in their place.

## Isolation steps

1. Agree the switching schedule with the Network Control Centre before any
   switching (see WE-SW-01).
2. Open the points of isolation and lock them off with a personal safety lock.
   Fit caution notices at every point of isolation.
3. Prove dead at the point of work with an approved voltage indicator. Prove
   the indicator on a known live source, or a proving unit, immediately before
   and immediately after the test.
4. Apply circuit main earths at the points of isolation, then drain earths at
   the point of work.
5. Receive the Permit to Work from the Authorised Person, read it, and sign
   for it. Work starts only then.

## Approach distances

Keep at least 0.8m from exposed live 11kV conductors and 1.0m from 33kV, unless
working under a Limitation of Access that states otherwise.

## When it goes wrong

If anyone is injured or there is danger to life, make the area safe without
putting yourself at risk, call 999, then call the Network Control Centre on the
radio or on 0306 999 0142.
