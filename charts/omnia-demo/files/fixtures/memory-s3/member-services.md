# Member services handbook

Members can ask for help with claims, mortgage applications, address changes,
and payment questions. Support staff should verify the member and case before
disclosing personal details. When a request crosses team boundaries, preserve
the original case reference and record the hand-off reason.

The service objective is a clear next step. If a policy answer depends on a
document that is not available, tell the member what is missing and who will
review it. Do not promise a settlement date until the responsible team has
accepted the case.
