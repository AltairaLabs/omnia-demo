# Claims handling guide

Larkmere Mutual reviews member claims using a delegated-authority model. Claims
up to £500 may be settled by an authorised assessor when the evidence is
complete. Claims above £500 require human approval before settlement.

The claims team records the claim reference, policy number, incident date,
supporting evidence, and the assessor's decision. A referral must explain which
authority threshold was exceeded and identify the human queue that owns the
next step.
